All claims about master and PRs are **source predictions**, not deployed-receiver observations. The local live probes are linked separately. ## Verification boundary

The current-branch converter and servlet suites passed on September 30 (97 and 243 cases respectively). The live probes above are separately linked and do not stand in for all 72 contracts. The local 42-case OpenLineage smoke selection hit the development wrapper's ten-minute process timeout after its first 16 cases passed. The other 26 finished in bounded groups: 24 passed and two failed at assertions that encode earlier B13/B14 policies; separate live sequences now cover the intended replacement and authority behavior. This is **40 observed passes, two test failures**, not a clean suite pass. No upstream master or PR-head service was deployed for this audit. For an `U` cell, the table gives the exact event/readback check still required. The [independent PR appendix](openlineage-153-independent-pr-facet-provenance-2026-10-01.md) identifies the heads whose receiver route or dependency differs; it does not combine those heads with the six-PR stack.
