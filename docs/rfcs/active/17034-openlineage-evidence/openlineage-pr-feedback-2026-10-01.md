# Feedback to DataHub: gaps remaining in the OpenLineage PRs

**Question:** Which gaps remain in your PRs against master, including bugs already fixed on our branch?

**Audit boundary (2026-10-01):** DataHub [master `318467e`](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/tree/318467eb125a281614c27de44f37b3f76695a0fd), the cumulative heads [#18175](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/pull/18175) → [#19733](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/pull/19733) → [#19734](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/pull/19734) → [#19736](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/pull/19736) → [#19740](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/pull/19740) → [#19800](https://github.com/datahub-project/datahub/pull/19800), our [dirty source snapshot](openlineage-goal-2026-10-01.snapshot.json), and OpenLineage [1.53.0 at `8ad5c14`](https://github.com/OpenLineage/OpenLineage/tree/8ad5c14c63fbab63fedd8ff42f9a208d86ad07fe/spec). The [snapshot](openlineage-goal-2026-10-01.snapshot.json) pins every head, including 27 independent PRs. Master moved since the original source inspection, but its selected OpenLineage source [digest did not change](openlineage-goal-pin-refresh-2026-10-01.md). **No master or PR-head receiver was deployed.** Upstream results below are source predictions. Our branch has [persisted readbacks and test results](openlineage-goal-progress-2026-09-30.md); they do not prove the remote heads.

## Independent PRs

The 27 [separately pinned independent heads](openlineage-153-independent-pr-facet-provenance-2026-10-01.md) are alternatives, not additions to the stack. Nineteen do not change this receiver facet route. The changed routes need individual checks: #19257 is a different receiver with a 1.45 catalog; #19419 adds a conditional SQL→Query path; #19422 changes symlink identity; #19573 upgrades the SDK; #13066, #17015, #18652, and #19952 change producer/identity gates. ## Evidence and test status

Our branch passed 97 converter, 243 servlet (including 122 fixture-corpus), 14 metadata-io, one focused GraphQL mapper, and three authentication tests. Of 42 selected OpenLineage smoke cases, **40 passed and two existing assertions failed**; the failing B13/B14 assertions encode older policies for native dependency edges and child-retained forwarded facets. Separate [B13](openlineage-goal-2026-10-01-live-dependency-ownership.json) and [B14](openlineage-goal-2026-10-01-live-forwarded-authority.json) persisted sequences cover the intended current behavior. This is **not a green smoke suite**. No product code or tests were changed for this audit, and no PR comments or tickets were created.
