## Exclusion boundary

The other 19 PRs do not edit the OpenLineage receiver facet route in their PR diffs. Some produce lineage through Spark, Airflow, or other connectors and matter to end-to-end producer behavior; they do not supply an alternative OpenLineage receiver implementation. Their head snapshots can inherit older receiver code, so an exact master verdict cannot be inferred solely from the absence of a changed receiver file.

No independent PR head was executed in this provenance audit. The bespoke heads remain `unverified` for their changed routes until converter output, persisted aspects, and read APIs are tested at those immutable revisions.
